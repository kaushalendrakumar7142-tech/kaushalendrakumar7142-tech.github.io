Abstractly Channel Manager

Abstractly Channel Manager is a private tool that the owner of the Abstractly YouTube channel uses to manage that one channel. It uploads videos, edits video details and playlists, replies to and moderates comments, and reads channel statistics.

It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign up for it. It works through the YouTube API Services and accesses only the channel whose owner signed in and gave permission.
